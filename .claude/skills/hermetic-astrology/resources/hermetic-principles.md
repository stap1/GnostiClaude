# Hermetic Principles — The Philosophical Frame

This is what distinguishes this skill from a purely technical chart reader. Western
astrology grew out of the **Hermetic tradition** — the body of teaching attributed to
*Hermes Trismegistus* ("thrice-great Hermes"), fusing Greek, Egyptian, and later
Renaissance thought (Ficino, Agrippa, the *Corpus Hermeticum*, the *Emerald Tablet*,
and the *Picatrix*). Use these principles to frame interpretation with depth and
dignity, never as dogma or fortune-telling.

## The Foundational Axiom

> **"Quod est superius est sicut quod est inferius."**
> *That which is above is like that which is below.* — The Emerald Tablet

The chart is a map of correspondence: the heavens mirror the psyche mirror the body
mirror the state. A planet is one power expressed simultaneously on every level
(metal, organ, hour, faculty of soul). Reading a chart is reading one integrated being,
not a list of separate influences.

## The Seven Hermetic Principles (the *Kybalion*, 1908 — a modern synthesis)

| # | Principle | Statement | Astrological application |
|---|-----------|-----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Mentalism** | "The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental." | The chart describes states of consciousness, not fixed fate. |
| 2 | **Correspondence** | "As above, so below; as below, so above." | Planet ↔ metal ↔ organ ↔ psyche form one chain (see planets.md). |
| 3 | **Vibration** | "Nothing rests; everything moves and vibrates." | Each planet is a frequency; aspects are resonance/dissonance. |
| 4 | **Polarity** | "Everything is dual; opposites are identical in nature." | Detriment/fall, oppositions, day/night sect — poles of one axis. |
| 5 | **Rhythm** | "Everything flows, out and in; the pendulum-swing." | Cycles, transits, returns, planetary hours — timing as tide. |
| 6 | **Cause & Effect** | "Every cause has its effect; nothing escapes law." | Saturn/karma; the chart shows tendencies and their consequences. |
| 7 | **Gender** | "Gender is in everything; masculine & feminine principles." | Diurnal/nocturnal signs, active/receptive planets and elements. |

> Note for accuracy: the *Kybalion* is an early-20th-century text, not ancient Hermetica.
> Present it as a useful modern framework, and reserve "classical Hermetic" for the
> *Corpus Hermeticum*, *Emerald Tablet*, and Renaissance sources.

## The Four Elements & Three Principles (alchemical layer)
- **Four Elements:** Fire, Earth, Air, Water — the temperaments and building blocks
  (see signs.md). The **quintessence** (aether) is their harmonizing fifth.
- **Three Alchemical Principles:** **Sulphur** (soul/Sun — the active, fiery essence),
  **Mercury** (spirit/Mercury — the mediating, volatile mind), **Salt** (body/Saturn —
  the fixed, material base). A balanced chart integrates all three.

## The Great Chain: Planet → Sign → House
Interpretation should always integrate the three levels rather than reading them
in isolation:
- **Planet** = the *power* (what force is acting).
- **Sign** = the *quality/medium* (how it is colored and moves).
- **House** = the *field* (where in life it manifests).
- **Aspect** = the *relationship* (how powers cooperate or contend).
- **Dignity + sect** = the *condition* (how freely and well it can act).

## Ethical & Interpretive Stance (mandatory)
1. **Free will over fatalism** (Principle of Mentalism). The chart shows tendencies,
   potentials, and timing — never a sentence. Speak of "trends," "invitations,"
   "the work of this placement," not "you will."
2. **The chart as mirror for self-knowledge** (*gnōthi seauton* — "know thyself,"
   inscribed at Delphi and central to the Hermetic path).
3. **No fear-based, deterministic, or medically/legally/financially prescriptive
   claims.** Offer reflection and meaning, not diagnosis or decree.
4. **Hold the tension of opposites** (Principle of Polarity): a difficult placement
   is also a source of strength; a comfortable one can breed complacency.
