# The Twelve Houses — Western System

Houses are the twelve fields of lived experience, measured from the **Ascendant**
(the degree rising on the eastern horizon at birth). Where the *signs* show HOW
energies express and the *planets* show WHAT is at work, the *houses* show WHERE —
in which arena of life — it plays out.

> **House systems.** The engine defaults to **Whole Sign** (traditional/
> Hellenistic — most robust with uncertain birth times) and also supports Equal,
> Porphyry, and Placidus (`W`/`E`/`O`/`P`; Placidus falls back to Porphyry when
> circumpolar — the effective system is in `meta.house_system_effective`). Koch
> and Regiomontanus are not implemented. If the **birth time is unknown or
> approximate, house placements and the Ascendant/MC are unreliable** — say so,
> and lean on sign/aspect analysis instead.

## The Twelve Houses

| House | Name | Domain | Type | Natural sign/ruler |
|-------|------|--------|------|--------------------|
| **1st** | Ascendant / Vita | Self, body, appearance, temperament, vitality | Angular | Aries / Mars |
| **2nd** | Lucrum | Money, possessions, values, resources, self-worth | Succedent | Taurus / Venus |
| **3rd** | Fratres | Mind, siblings, communication, short trips, learning | Cadent | Gemini / Mercury |
| **4th** | Genitor (IC) | Home, family, roots, father/lineage, endings, land | Angular | Cancer / Moon |
| **5th** | Nati | Creativity, children, romance, play, self-expression | Succedent | Leo / Sun |
| **6th** | Valetudo | Work, service, health, routine, skills, subordinates | Cadent | Virgo / Mercury |
| **7th** | Uxor (DSC) | Marriage, partnership, open enemies, contracts, the Other | Angular | Libra / Venus |
| **8th** | Mors | Death/rebirth, shared resources, sex, the occult, crisis | Succedent | Scorpio / Mars |
| **9th** | Iter | Higher mind, philosophy, religion, long travel, law | Cadent | Sagittarius / Jupiter |
| **10th** | Regnum (MC) | Career, vocation, reputation, authority, the aim of life | Angular | Capricorn / Saturn |
| **11th** | Benefacta | Friends, groups, hopes, allies, gains, the collective | Succedent | Aquarius / Saturn |
| **12th** | Carcer | The hidden, solitude, the unconscious, loss, transcendence | Cadent | Pisces / Jupiter |

## House Strength (accidental dignity)

| Type | Houses | Strength |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Angular** (Kendra-equivalent) | 1, 4, 7, 10 | Strongest — planets here act forcefully and visibly |
| **Succedent** | 2, 5, 8, 11 | Moderate — stabilizing, resourceful |
| **Cadent** | 3, 6, 9, 12 | Weakest — diffuse, mental, transitional |

- **Joys** (traditional): Mercury joys in the 1st, Moon in the 3rd, Venus in the 5th,
  Mars in the 6th, Sun in the 9th, Jupiter in the 11th, Saturn in the 12th.

## The Angles (most important points)
- **ASC (1st cusp)** — the self, the body, the point of incarnation.
- **IC (4th cusp)** — roots, home, the private foundation, the end of the matter.
- **DSC (7th cusp)** — the partner, the significant Other, open opponents.
- **MC (10th cusp)** — vocation, public role, what one is known for.

## Reading a House
For each house consider, in order:
1. **Planets inside it** — direct activation of that life area.
2. **The sign on the cusp** — the "style" or coloring of that area.
3. **The ruler of the cusp sign, and where THAT planet sits** — this "carries" the
   house's affairs to another part of the chart (dispositor logic). E.g. the 10th
   ruler in the 5th → career expressed through creativity or children.
4. **Aspects to planets in the house and to the ruler.**

**Priority houses** for most readings: 1 (self), 7 (relationship), 10 (vocation),
4 (home/roots), plus whichever house holds the **chart ruler** (the Ascendant's ruler).
