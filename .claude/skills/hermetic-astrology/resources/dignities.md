# Essential Dignities — Traditional Planetary Strength

Essential dignity measures how much a planet is "at home" in its zodiacal position —
its ability to act with clarity and produce its natural effects. It is built on the
**five dignities** of Ptolemy and William Lilly. The engine computes all five plus
a numeric score.

## The Five Essential Dignities (with Lilly's scoring)

| Dignity | Score | Meaning |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Domicile (Rulership)** | +5 | Planet in the sign it rules — fully empowered, "at home" |
| **Exaltation** | +4 | Planet as honored guest — strong, sometimes exaggerated |
| **Triplicity** | +3 | Planet rules the element of the sign (by sect) — comfortable |
| **Term (Bound)** | +2 | Planet rules the degree-band (Egyptian terms) — minor support |
| **Face (Decan)** | +1 | Planet rules the 10° decan (Chaldean faces) — faint dignity |

## Debilities

| Debility | Score | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Detriment** | −5 | Planet in the sign opposite its domicile — out of place, strained |
| **Fall** | −4 | Planet in the sign opposite its exaltation — weakened, undervalued |
| **Peregrine** | −5 | Planet with NO essential dignity at its position — "wandering," rudderless |

> **Peregrine stacks with Detriment/Fall** (Lilly): a planet in detriment that also
> holds no dignity is Detriment + Peregrine = −10; in fall with no dignity, −9.
> The engine scores it this way. A debilitated planet that still rules its own
> term or face is NOT peregrine — the minor dignity gives it a "foothold."

| Element | Signs | Day ruler | Night ruler | Participating |
|---------|-------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Sun | Jupiter | Saturn |
| Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Venus | Moon | Mars |
| Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Saturn | Mercury | Jupiter |
| Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Mars | Mars | Moon |

> **Sect matters:** use the day ruler in a day chart (Sun above the horizon) and the
> night ruler in a night chart. The engine determines sect from the Sun's true
> altitude and scores triplicity by this table (Lilly's scheme, where Mars rules
> Water both day and night). The older **Dorothean** scheme differs only for Water:
> Venus by day, Mars by night, Moon participating — mention it if the querent asks.

## Faces (Decans) — see decans.md
The 36 faces (10° each) follow the Chaldean order beginning with Mars on Aries 0°.
A planet in its own face has +1 dignity.

## Accidental Dignity (condition, not zodiacal)
Essential dignity says whether a planet is strong *in itself*; **accidental dignity**
says whether it is well-*placed and conditioned*:
- **Angular** house (1/4/7/10) strengthens; **cadent** (3/6/9/12) weakens.
- **Direct & swift** motion strengthens; **retrograde / stationary / slow** weakens.
- **Free of the Sun's beams** strengthens; **combust** weakens; **cazimi** strengthens.
- **Aspected by benefics** (Jupiter, Venus) helps; **afflicted by malefics** (Mars, Saturn) hurts.
- **In its joy** or on an angle helps.

## Interpreting the dignity score
- **+5 or higher** — very strong; the planet reliably delivers its significations.
- **+1 to +4** — functional; works with some support.
- **0 / peregrine** — neutral-to-weak; needs help from house, aspect, or reception.
- **negative (detriment/fall)** — impeded; the planet struggles and needs conscious work.
Always temper the essential score with **accidental** condition and **sect** before
pronouncing a planet "strong" or "weak."
