# Closing-Quote Bank — Nag Hammadi (Step-8 closer, mandatory style rule)

**Do NOT close a synthesis with the Hermetic motto** („Jak na górze, tak na dole;
poznaj samego siebie") — it already appears in the standard disclaimer footer, and
repeating it as the Step-8 closer reads as a tic. End the synthesis with ONE apt
quote from the bank below, chosen to match the reading's dominant theme, with a
short attribution. Vary the quote across a person's readings. The motto remains
ONLY in the footer and (optionally) as the "Jak na górze, tak na dole —" opener
of Step 6.

Source: *Biblioteka z Nag Hammadi*, tłum. Wincenty Myszor (PDF in the project
root: `APOKRYFY BIBLIOTEKA Z NAG HAMMADI.pdf`); quotes keep Myszor's Polish wording.
