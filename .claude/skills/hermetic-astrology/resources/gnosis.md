# Gnosis — the Christian-Gnostic Layer of the Hermetic Reading

An optional interpretive frame that deepens the skill's motto — *"as above, so below;
know thyself"* — with its historical sibling: **Christian Gnosticism** (2nd–3rd c.:
Valentinus, Basilides, the Sethians; primary texts in the **Nag Hammadi library**,
found 1945). Gnosticism and Hermetism grew side by side in Hellenistic Alexandria and
share one axiom: what liberates is **gnosis** — knowledge of who one really is — not
belief or observance alone. Use this layer to give a reading contemplative depth;
never present it as theology, prediction, or a claim about the user's faith.

## The Descent: Planetary "Garments"

Both traditions tell the same astral myth: descending toward birth, the soul passes
through the **seven planetary spheres** and in each puts on a "garment" — a faculty
that serves life but can also enslave when unexamined. In the Hermetic *Poimandres*
(Corpus Hermeticum I) the ascending soul **returns each gift to its sphere**:

| Sphere | Garment received (CH I.25 — the unexamined face) | Redeemed face (integrated) |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| ☽ Moon | growth & diminution — reactive moods, clinging | responsive care, living rhythm |
| ☿ Mercury | cunning, machination | honest intellect, true speech |
| ♀ Venus | illusion of desire | love that sees, taste, concord |
| ☉ Sun | arrogance of rulership | sovereign purpose, generosity |
| ♂ Mars | rash audacity | courage with measure, right effort |
| ♃ Jupiter | greed for wealth | magnanimity, wisdom that provides |
| ♄ Saturn | ensnaring falsehood | truth borne patiently, structure |

**Application to dignities:** the natal chart is the inventory of garments as this
soul wears them. A planet in domicile/exaltation ≈ a garment already half-integrated;
detriment/fall/peregrine ≈ a garment worn inside-out — it rules invisibly until
recognized. Never phrase this as guilt: the garment is *equipment for incarnation*,
and the reading's task is recognition, not removal.

## Heimarmene: Fate That Binds Only the Unawakened

Gnostic Christians did not dismiss astrology — they bounded it. The spheres' rulers
(**archons**) administer **heimarmene**, compulsive fate; but gnosis steps outside
their jurisdiction:

> "Until baptism, they say, fate is real; but after it the astrologers no longer
> speak the truth." — *Excerpta ex Theodoto* 78 (Clement of Alexandria)

> In the *Pistis Sophia*, the risen Christ "turns the spheres", so that the powers
> "no longer know the times" — the awakened are unreadable to the machinery of fate.

**This is the ancient root of the skill's mandatory non-fatalism.** "A map of
tendencies, not a verdict" is not a modern disclaimer bolted on — it is Theodotus'
doctrine restated: the chart describes the mechanics of the *unexamined* life with
real accuracy, and loses jurisdiction exactly where consciousness enters. A planet
read and owned stops acting as an archon (compulsion) and starts acting as a power.

## Anamnesis: the Reading as Remembering

The gnostic path is less "learning" than **anamnesis** — remembering who one is:

- **Gospel of Thomas, log. 3:** "When you know yourselves, then you will be known…
  If you do not know yourselves, you dwell in poverty."
- **Hymn of the Pearl** (Acts of Thomas): the prince sent for the pearl drinks the
  food of Egypt, forgets his origin — until a letter from home wakes him *by name*.
- **Valentinian formula** (*Exc. Theod.* 78,2): liberating knowledge is "who we were,
  what we became; where we were, whither we were thrown; whither we hasten, whence
  we are redeemed; what birth is, and what rebirth."

**Application:** frame a natal reading as the "letter from home" — it names the
garments so the wearer remembers being distinct from them. Hard transits of slow
planets (Saturn, the outers) read naturally as *the sphere knocking*: a period when
one garment is held to the light. The synthesis can close on the Valentinian
questions turned gently to the chart: what did this soul put on (dignities), where
was it thrown (houses, sect), whither does it hasten (nodes, ⊗ Spirit, MC).

## Tone & Accuracy Rules (mandatory when this layer is used)

- Offer the gnostic frame as **symbol and contemplation**, opt-in, never as doctrine;
  respect the user's own faith and vocabulary. Historic Gnosticism was heterodox
  (opposed by Irenaeus and Hippolytus) — present it as a historical current, not as
  "the true Christianity".
- Keep the skill's language rules: no fatalism, no fear. In this frame that means:
  archons are **not** enemies but unowned faculties; heimarmene ends where the
  reading succeeds.
- Attribute accurately: *Poimandres*/CH — Hermetic; Nag Hammadi texts, *Pistis
  Sophia*, Theodotus — Christian-Gnostic; the two are siblings, not one school.
  Scholarly anchors: H. Jonas *The Gnostic Religion*, K. Rudolph *Gnosis*; Polish
  translations of Nag Hammadi by Wincenty Myszor.
