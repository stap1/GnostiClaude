# Decans (Faces) — The 36 Divisions

Each sign divides into three **decans** of 10° each, giving 36 decans around the
zodiac. Decans are one of the oldest layers of astrology (Egyptian in origin) and a
core Hermetic device — the *Picatrix* and Agrippa assign images, spirits, and magical
virtues to each. They add fine-grained nuance beneath the sign level.

## Two decan systems (both valid; use per purpose)

### 1. Chaldean Faces (traditional / Hermetic — used by the engine for dignity)
Decan rulers follow the **Chaldean order** of planets
(Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon) beginning with **Mars on Aries 0°–10°**,
then cycling continuously through all 36 faces:

| Sign | Decan I (0–10°) | Decan II (10–20°) | Decan III (20–30°) |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Aries | Mars | Sun | Venus |
| Taurus | Mercury | Moon | Saturn |
| Gemini | Jupiter | Mars | Sun |
| Cancer | Venus | Mercury | Moon |
| Leo | Saturn | Jupiter | Mars |
| Virgo | Sun | Venus | Mercury |
| Libra | Moon | Saturn | Jupiter |
| Scorpio | Mars | Sun | Venus |
| Sagittarius | Mercury | Moon | Saturn |
| Capricorn | Jupiter | Mars | Sun |
| Aquarius | Venus | Mercury | Moon |
| Pisces | Saturn | Jupiter | Mars |

A planet in its own face receives +1 essential dignity (see dignities.md).

### 2. Triplicity Decans (modern / "sub-signs")
Each decan is sub-ruled by a sign of the **same element**, in order:
- **1st decan** → ruled by the sign itself (pure expression).
- **2nd decan** → the next sign of that element.
- **3rd decan** → the last sign of that element.

Example (Fire): Aries decans → Aries / Leo / Sagittarius sub-tones. So 25° Aries
carries a Sagittarius flavor (adventurous, philosophical) over the Aries base.

## How to use decans
- **Placement nuance:** name the decan of the Sun, Moon, and Ascendant for a finer
  character sketch than the sign alone.
- **Dignity:** the Chaldean face ruler contributes to the essential-dignity score.
- **Hermetic/magical context:** if the querent is interested in talismanic or
  ceremonial correspondences, the decan's planetary ruler is the operative power
  (see hermetic-principles.md and planetary-hours.md for election).

> When labeling the output directory's "zodiac sign," use the **Sun sign** by default;
> note the decan in the reading body for extra precision.
