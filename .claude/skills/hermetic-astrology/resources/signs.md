# The Twelve Signs — Tropical Zodiac

The **tropical** zodiac (Western/European) is measured from the vernal equinox
(0° Aries = the Sun's position at the spring equinox), NOT from the fixed stars.
This is the key difference from the sidereal zodiac. No ayanamsa is applied.

## The Four Elements (Triplicities)

| Element | Signs | Nature | Temperament | Keyword |
|---------|-------|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Fire** | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Hot & dry | Choleric | Spirit, will, inspiration |
| **Earth** | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Cold & dry | Melancholic | Body, practicality, substance |
| **Air** | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Hot & moist | Sanguine | Mind, relation, ideas |
| **Water** | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Cold & moist | Phlegmatic | Soul, feeling, intuition |

## The Three Modalities (Quadruplicities)

| Modality | Signs | Function |
|----------|-------|----------|
| **Cardinal** | Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn | Initiate — begin, launch, lead |
| **Fixed** | Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius | Sustain — stabilize, hold, persist |
| **Mutable** | Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces | Adapt — change, distribute, transition |

## Polarity
- **Diurnal / Positive / Masculine:** the Fire and Air signs (odd signs) — outward, expressive.
- **Nocturnal / Negative / Feminine:** the Earth and Water signs (even signs) — inward, receptive.

## Sign Keywords (for interpretation)

- **Aries** — initiative, courage, assertion, impatience, "I am."
- **Taurus** — stability, sensuality, persistence, possessiveness, "I have."
- **Gemini** — curiosity, versatility, communication, restlessness, "I think."
- **Cancer** — nurture, memory, protection, moodiness, "I feel."
- **Leo** — creativity, pride, generosity, drama, "I create."
- **Virgo** — analysis, service, refinement, criticism, "I analyze / serve."
- **Libra** — harmony, fairness, relationship, indecision, "I relate / balance."
- **Scorpio** — depth, intensity, transformation, control, "I desire / transform."
- **Sagittarius** — vision, freedom, faith, excess, "I seek / understand."
- **Capricorn** — ambition, structure, mastery, coldness, "I build / achieve."
- **Aquarius** — innovation, ideals, detachment, rebellion, "I know / liberate."
- **Pisces** — compassion, imagination, transcendence, escapism, "I dream / believe."

## How signs modify planets
A planet expresses its **nature** (planets.md) through the **medium** of the sign:
element colors *how* the energy flows, modality colors *how* it moves, and the sign's
ruler ties it back into the chart's web of dispositors. A planet in its own sign or
exaltation acts with dignity and ease; in detriment or fall it is strained and must
work harder (see dignities.md).
